# djsync
Welcome to DJSync 🎧

As a DJ, preparing your USB drive shouldn't be a hassle. DJSync is a standalone desktop app designed to bridge the gap between your favorite streaming platforms and your Rekordbox USB.

Whether you want to import a full Spotify playlist, search for a specific track on YouTube Music, or grab a remix from SoundCloud, DJSync handles everything. It automatically downloads the audio, tags it, and safely injects it directly into your Rekordbox database (exportLibrary.db)—all from a clean, dark-mode interface. Spend less time managing files and more time mixing!

# Code of conduct

DJSync is a small, free project made by DJs for DJs. Everyone who takes part — opening an issue,
testing a release, sending a pull request, answering a question — is expected to keep it a friendly
and useful place.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with different levels of experience, different gear and
  different languages (English and French are both welcome here).
- **Stay on topic and constructive.** Describe problems with facts (version, system, steps, log),
  criticise ideas and code, never people.
- **Respect privacy.** Never post someone else's personal information. Do not attach your own
  Rekordbox library, USB drive contents or logs that contain names you do not want to share
  (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Respect the law and artists.** DJSync is not a place to share copyrighted music or links to it.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or remarks (about origin, gender, sexual orientation,
disability, age, religion, appearance or anything else), personal attacks, threats, sexualised
content, spam and deliberate disruption.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and may block people who keep doing so. Decisions are made case by case, as fairly as possible.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, open a short issue saying you would like to be contacted
privately, without details, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Security
problems follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

This code of conduct is inspired by the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), written in our own words.
